What is Sales CRM ServiceNow Sales CRM manages the whole product sales life cycle on one platform: pre-sales leads and opportunities, quotes, order capture and fulfilment, order and invoice cases, and post-sales changes and renewals. Front, middle and back-office work from the same data, so there is no hand-off between a separate CRM, CPQ and order system. Companies have long run these steps across separate stacks: a CRM for selling, a CPQ tool for quoting, an order manager for fulfilment and back-office systems for billing and service.
